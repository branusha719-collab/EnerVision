# EnerVision

## AI-Powered Renewable Energy Forecasting Platform

### Overview

EnerVision is an AI-driven renewable energy forecasting platform designed to predict solar and wind power generation using weather parameters and historical generation data.

The system aims to help energy planners and grid operators make informed decisions by providing accurate forecasts, confidence intervals, and explainable insights.

---

## Problem Statement

Renewable energy sources such as solar and wind are highly dependent on weather conditions. Variations in temperature, wind speed, humidity, cloud cover, and solar irradiance can significantly impact power generation.

Accurate forecasting of renewable energy generation can improve:

* Grid stability
* Energy scheduling
* Resource planning
* Decision making

---

## Project Objectives

* Forecast solar and wind energy generation using machine learning.
* Analyze the impact of weather conditions on renewable power output.
* Provide prediction confidence intervals (P10, P50, P90).
* Generate explainable forecasts using AI explainability techniques.
* Visualize forecasts through an interactive dashboard.
